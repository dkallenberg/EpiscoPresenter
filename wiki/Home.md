# EpiscoPresenter and EpiscoPlanner

Two desktop apps (Mac and Windows) for Episcopal worship:

* **EpiscoPlanner** is for planning. The rector, parish administrator,
  musician or technician use it on their own computers to plan services,
  choose hymns and readings, and make the **bulletin** and the
  **officiant's script**.
* **EpiscoPresenter** runs the service on Sunday. It drives the
  **sanctuary screen** and the **live-stream overlay**, from one computer or
  from two computers kept in step.

Both apps share one **parish library folder** (for example a shared Google
Drive folder), so a service planned in EpiscoPlanner is ready on the
presentation computer on Sunday morning.

## What the apps include, and what they don't

* **Included:** the texts of the 1979 Book of Common Prayer (public
  domain): the Holy Eucharist in Rite I and Rite II, the Psalter, the
  collects, Holy Week, baptism and ordination services. Also the Revised
  Common Lectionary (Years A, B and C, Tracks 1 and 2, holy days) and the
  church calendar.
* **Not included:** copyrighted texts. Bible readings (NRSV, NRSVue), hymn
  words (the Hymnal 1982) and supplemental liturgies such as *Enriching Our
  Worship* come from the parish's own licensed copies. The apps make it
  quick to paste or import them, and they stay in the parish library
  folder.

Please proofread the built-in texts against your Prayer Book before relying
on them in worship.
