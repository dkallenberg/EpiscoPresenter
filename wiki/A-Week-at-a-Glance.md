# A week at a glance

A typical week for a parish with an 8:00 and a 10:30 service.

## Early in the week (EpiscoPlanner)

1. **☰ Services.** If the coming Sunday isn't listed, click **Schedule
   services… → The coming Sundays**. The services are made with the day's
   name, colour, collect, readings and psalm already in place.
2. **Open the service** and look at **To do** beside the bulletin. It lists
   what's still needed, and each line opens the place to fix it:
   * **Reading texts.** Click **Reading texts…** and either import the
     parish's Bible file once (then every reading fills itself in) or paste
     each reading. See [Bulletin and script](Bulletin-and-Script#reading-texts).
   * **Hymns.** Select a hymn slot (*Entrance Hymn*, *Offertory Hymn*…).
     Pick a hymn in the **Library** and click **Use for Entrance Hymn**, or
     paste its words in the **Editor**. See [Editing items](Editing-Items#hymns).
   * **The preacher.** Select **The Sermon** and choose the preacher in the
     Editor.
3. Make any other changes: a different Eucharistic Prayer, a seasonal
   blessing, an announcement slide, a video. See
   [Editing items](Editing-Items) and
   [Slides, events and video](Slides-Events-and-Video).
4. On the **Bulletin** tab, click **Export to Word**, check it, and print.
5. On the **Script** tab, **Export to Word** makes the officiant's copy.

## By Saturday (EpiscoPlanner)

6. On the **Services** screen, click **Mark Ready** on each service. The
   button turns green (**✓ Ready**), and within a minute the service is
   copied to the presentation computer with its pictures and videos.

## Sunday morning (EpiscoPresenter)

7. Open EpiscoPresenter. The service is listed under **From the planner**.
   Open it.
8. Open the screens with the **monitor icon** at the right end of the top
   bar.
9. Before the service, the announcements loop runs on the screens.
10. Press **→** (or **Next**) to step through the service. See
    [Running a service](Running-a-Service).

Changes made in the planner after a service is marked ready are copied over
too, unless the service is open on the presentation computer. Then
**⟳ Updated in the planner** appears in the top bar: click it when it's safe
to take the planner's version.
